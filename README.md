# Candy World

A sugary new star system for Outer Wilds, built with [New Horizons](https://outerwildsmods.com/mods/newhorizons/).

Explore **Candyland**, a pink planet wrapped in a chocolate river shaped like the seam of a tennis ball, dotted with candy canes and cotton-candy trees, orbiting a cotton-candy sun.

## The mystery

Two Nomai, MGS and HNS, found a strange gingerbread house at the north pole. Inside, four striped pillars surround a sealed chocolate trapdoor. Find the four candy tablets hidden around the planet, place them on the pillars, and see what lies beneath.

Explore:
- **The Candy Grove** - a forest of lollipop trees ringed by giant candy pebbles
- **Peppermint Ruins** - shattered peppermints, broken candy canes and toppled frosted columns
- **The Red Velvet Volcano** - a towering cake volcano at the south pole (bring your jetpack!)
- **The Gumdrop Caves** - a fudge maze hidden under a mound of gumdrops

Tip: your signalscope can pick up the tablets on a brand new frequency.

## Installation

Install with the [Outer Wilds Mod Manager](https://outerwildsmods.com/mod-manager/). Requires New Horizons.
